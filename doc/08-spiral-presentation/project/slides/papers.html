Fill the list with the papers you mean (title, venue, year). Then: most of them are either impractical or plain wrong. One representative wrong claim: you need a thread pool to manage I/O and saturate the bandwidth. Our number: one I/O thread drove 12 instance SSDs on the i7i.48xlarge at 47.8 GiB/s, 94% of fio's 50.1 GiB/s; the drives under t4 read 48,475 MiB/s (doc/04-multi-device.md, Twelve faster drives). Be honest about the edge: at that rate the one worker is full, 93% of its samples in io_uring_enter, so one worker submits about 48 GiB/s of these reads and no more. Say, don't write: most of these papers are written by junior PhDs under paper pressure, so take their conclusions with a grain of salt.
