While they wait, GETs for adjacent bytes of the same file merge into one GET of up to 4 MiB: a's blocks 0 to 2 become one 3 MiB GET, b's 4 and 5 one of 2 MiB. Fewer GETs, each about as fast as a small one on an object store. A merge takes the first one's place in the line. On by default since 27 Oct (T4CachingBuilder::DEFAULT_MERGED_UP_TO); merged up to 4 MiB under the old GET cap they cut the GETs by 28% and the queries' time by 6.5% (doc/06-read-modes.md).
