INTERNAL · CLICKBENCH, VORTEX, 2 MiB DICTIONARIES · c6in.8xlarge 26 OCT · i7i.48xlarge 24 OCT 2026 · doc/06

Card 1, c6in.8xlarge at 50 Gbit/s, 26 Oct, Vortex read raw through t4: capping in-flight GETs at about 200 MB cut the GET p99, wait included, from 448 to 281 ms and the slowest GET from 3.4 s to 0.93 s. Card 2: merging waiting GETs up to 4 MiB under that cap cut GETs by 28% and query time by 6.5%. Card 3, i7i.48xlarge at 100 Gbit/s (a 400 MB cap), queries 20 to 34, cap plus merging plus racing at the p90: Vortex's raw reads had a slowest GET of 0.82 s and none over 1 s, against 3.3 s and 166 without the queue (24 Oct). With Vortex's own merged reads, which the bench uses now: slowest 1.13 s, 23 over 1 s. Be upfront that Parquet got worse on the i7i, slowest 3.9 s and 12,963 over 1 s: its 4 MiB GETs hold a connection longer than the 32 ms the cap is sized for. A cap that accounts for connection time is open (doc/06-read-modes.md, decision 11).
