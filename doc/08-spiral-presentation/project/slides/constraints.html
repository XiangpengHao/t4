What t4 will not give up

01

Any async runtime

t4 runs under tokio or any other executor. It owns one I/O thread and assumes no runtime. You pass in a timer.

02

TLS as it is

Remote reads use standard TLS. No home-made encryption in its place to save CPU.

The constraints t4 works under, and most papers don't. One: it must be runtime agnostic. You can't say tokio is not supported. t4 keeps one I/O thread of its own and never assumes an executor; a late race needs a timer, which the user gives with T4CachingBuilder::with_sleep (doc/06-read-modes.md, decision 7). Two: you can't invent your own encryption and replace TLS to win a benchmark.
