t4 caps the bytes it has in flight at what fills the NIC: the NIC's rate times what an unloaded GET takes, 50 Gbit/s times 32 ms, 200 MB by default (src/cache/get_queue.rs). Here the cap holds 5 MiB: a's 3 MiB GET and c's 1 MiB go; b's 2 MiB would pass the cap, so it waits at the front. More in flight than the NIC can carry only makes every GET slower, the slowest most.
