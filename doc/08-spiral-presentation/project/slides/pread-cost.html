Left is your own thought experiment from the outline: with 100 GB/s of memory bandwidth and 50 GB/s of SSDs, pread's copies alone ask for 150 GB/s, so just scanning the data uses up the machine. That is extremely wasteful. Right is what we measured on t4 itself. With copies on its read path, 12 SSDs gave about 13 GiB/s (12.5 to 13.3 GiB/s): a profile put zeroing buffers at about a third of the reading tasks' time, memmove at more than a quarter, and page faults at about a fifth. Reading into the caller's buffer, with mimalloc so pages stay mapped, gave 34.6 GiB/s, 98% of the drives' cap (doc/04-multi-device.md, Twelve drives and Reading straight into the output).
