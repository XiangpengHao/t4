What a block costs: finding it and reading it. If asked where Kismet is ahead today: it keeps its files across a restart, and every Spiral process on the machine shares the folder. t4 starts cold after a restart until the snapshot lands, and one process owns a cache file, with the others reading through it in shared mode, which is being built (doc/10 decisions 7 and 8, doc/11). Kismet's overshoot is its own words: caches grow past their capacity, rarely by more than a factor of 2 or 3 (kismet-cache lib.rs). t4's budget is never exceeded.
