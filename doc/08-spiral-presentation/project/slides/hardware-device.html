Memory: one server module, channel bandwidth divided by module price. 2008 to 2018: the fastest server memory speed of the year. 2008 DDR2-800 FB-DIMM 1 GB $84.99 (Crucial, Wayback 2008-05-06): 75. 2010 DDR3-1333 4 GB $169.99 (Crucial, 2010-08-08): 63. 2012 DDR3-1600 8 GB $99.99 (Crucial, 2012-05-11): 128. 2014 DDR4-2133 4 GB $68.99 (Crucial, 2014-10-06): 247. 2016 DDR4-2400 8 GB $69.99 (Crucial, 2016-02-28): 274. 2018 DDR4-2666 4 GB $60.99 (Crucial, 2018-08-20): 350. From 2020 the line stays on the same 8 GB DDR4-3200 RDIMM (Micron MTA9ASF1G72PZ, 25.6 GB/s a channel), so the module size doesn't change under it: 2020 $58.99 (Crucial, 2020-09-19): 434. 2022 $56.05 (Crucial, 2022-08-10): 457. 2024 $35.86 (Crucial, 2024-06-13): 714. Why DDR4: new servers since 2022 take only DDR5, whose smallest server module is 16 GB, so its cheapest module per channel costs more: DDR5-4800 16 GB $148 in 2022 (259), DDR5-6400 32 GB $194 in 2024 (264). 2026 is left out: the 2025 to 2026 DRAM price spike and DDR4's end of life make it a different story (DDR5-6400 32 GB $1,459 in October 2026).
