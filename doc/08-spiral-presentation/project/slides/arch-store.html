t4's index. Draw attention to the tree: it's a bbtree, a B+ tree. The key is (file, block): file a's version and a 1 MiB block number, packed into one u64, 40 bits of version and 24 of block. Its leaves hold one entry for each block t4 caches; the dashed ones, blocks 4 and 5, have no entry. There's a second bbtree, paths, that maps a file's path to its version (doc/03-cache.md, decision 4). This picture stays for the next slides.
