Builds in five steps. 1: your thread makes a system call. 2: the SSD writes the data into the kernel's page cache by DMA. 3: the CPU copies it from the page cache into your buffer. 4 and 5: what each costs. This is pread, and it is the tokio way of doing file I/O: tokio::fs runs a blocking pread on a thread pool. object_store's LocalFileSystem zeroes a buffer for each range and preads into it, so every byte is written twice (doc/04-multi-device.md, At 56 streams). The two costs are placeholders: measure a syscall and a 4 KiB memcpy on the bench box before the talk.
