The same model, query by query, summed over each suite: each query takes its time from memory, or its bytes off the SSDs at their steady rate, whichever is more. The bytes are what t4 read off the drives; the steady rate is fio's, ten seconds of 1 MiB reads after the burst. On the i3en.2xlarge, 1.36 GB/s, 7 of 22 TPC-H queries and 3 of 15 ClickBench queries are bound by the drives, and the model says 17.11 s and 43.15 s against t4's measured 16.79 s and 43.68 s. On the i7ie.12xlarge only three TPC-H queries touch the drives' limit and the model is memory's time give or take 1%; on the i7i.24xlarge no query does. There t4 runs as fast as memory or up to 5% faster: Vortex from memory pays for the page cache's own copies. Medians of the last five of twelve runs back to back, 28 Oct 2026.
