Keep this gentle. io_uring is fairly new: Linux 5.1, 2019. Your program and the kernel share two rings in memory, which is why they sit on the line between user space and the kernel: the submission queue, SQ, carries your reads in; the completion queue, CQ, carries results back. Same three lanes and the same two costs as the pread slides; watch how both shrink. tokio can't do this: tokio::fs runs a blocking pread on a thread pool. Vortex's local reader, FileReadAt, also preads on a blocking pool; vortex-duckdb chose it over tokio's pool, which it calls very bad for local reads on high-core machines (doc/09-vortex-integration.md).
