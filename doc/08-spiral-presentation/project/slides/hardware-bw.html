Their SSD figure is our own fio, 1 MiB direct reads on all drives as one RAID0, at the steady rate once the drives' burst after a rest is spent (dashboard t4 Disk Tier Across Boxes); ScyllaDB's iotune agrees within 5% (1.33, 12.2 and 25.6 GB/s). The point for the talk: a core gets a few GB/s of memory bandwidth as its share, while one instance's SSDs read tens of GB/s; on the i7i.24xlarge the six SSDs read 26.9 GB/s, the share of more than four of its 48 cores. The small i3en.2xlarge is the exception: four cores on a shared host pull 12.7 GB/s each while neighbours are quiet, and its two SSDs hold only 1.36 GB/s. Not plotted, no public SSD measurement: i7i, i7ie, i8g and i8ge at 48xlarge (our earlier fio read 53.8 GB/s on a two-socket i7i.48xlarge), c6id/m6id/r6id, x2idn, c7gd, c8gd, c8id/m8id/r8id.
