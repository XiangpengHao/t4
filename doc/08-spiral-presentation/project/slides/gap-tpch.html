The dashboard's toggle, as two clicks: TPC-H, then ClickBench. Each bar is a suite's summed median runs over Vortex's from memory on the same instance; the dashed line at 1 is Vortex from memory. Grey is Vortex reading the same files from the SSDs, its pages evicted before every run; blue is t4 from its disk tier on the same SSDs. TPC-H: Vortex from the SSDs is 25% behind memory on the 4-core instance and 10 to 15% on the bigger two; t4 is 5% behind on the small one, even on the 24-core one, and 4% ahead on the 48-core one.
