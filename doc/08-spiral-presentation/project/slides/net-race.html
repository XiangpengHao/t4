MODEL ON 24 OCT 2026 CLICKBENCH GETS, UNCAPPED · RaceStragglers · doc/06, decision 8

A GET that answers but is slow gets raced, once. t4 times every GET from its first request to its body all in, keeps the latest 64, and if a GET of a normal read is still running past their p90, it sends one more request for the bytes not in yet; whichever has them all first is taken, the other dropped (RaceStragglers, src/cache/policy/stragglers.rs; PlannedGet::with_late_race). Until it has heard 64 GETs it races at 500 ms, so a fresh cache's first GETs are covered too. It needs a timer, which the user gives with T4CachingBuilder::with_sleep; at the p90 it is the default read policy. A GET that never answers is retried by object_store, not raced. The numbers on the right are a model on 24 Oct's ClickBench GETs, uncapped: p99 495 ms, not 633, slowest 1.0 s, not 3.3, for 10% more GETs and 8 to 10% more bytes. The slow GETs come in bursts when bytes in flight are most, which racing adds to, and the model can't say what that costs. Racing adds GETs, and slow GETs come when the NIC is busiest: next, how t4 keeps its GETs from swamping it.
