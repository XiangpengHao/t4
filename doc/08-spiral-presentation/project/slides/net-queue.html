Builds one stage at a time. 1: reads go into a queue, first come, first served. 2: waiting GETs for a file's adjacent bytes merge into one GET of up to 4 MiB. 3: t4 caps the bytes it has in flight at what fills the NIC: the NIC's rate times what an unloaded GET takes, 50 Gbit/s times 32 ms, 200 MB by default (src/cache/get_queue.rs). Past the cap a GET waits its turn. 4: a GET slower than the p90 of the last 64 GET times is raced: t4 sends it again and keeps whichever answer comes first; until it has heard 64 GETs it races at 500 ms (src/cache/policy/stragglers.rs). A late racer goes at once, beside the cap.
