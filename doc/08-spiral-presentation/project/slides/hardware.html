Price per GB, nominal dollars, the lowest retail price recorded each year (price divided by labelled capacity). 2006 to July 2024: John C. McCallum's tables of NewEgg consumer listings, DRAM DIMMs and mostly SATA SSDs (web.archive.org/web/20250716092935/https://jcmit.net/memoryprice.htm and web.archive.org/web/20250716094252/https://jcmit.net/flashprice.htm; jcmit.net itself no longer serves them). 2025 and 2026 (January to September): Stanford DAM's price tracker, the cheapest Amazon listing via Keepa, NVMe drives only (dam.stanford.edu/memory-prices.html). The two baskets differ, so read the 2024 to 2025 step loosely. McCallum's SSD series starts in 2013; no sourced retail SSD price before that (one enterprise point: Intel X25-E, 2008, $21.7 per GB in 1,000-unit lots). Memory: $74.50 in 2006, $1.31 in 2023, $2.83 in 2026, the cheapest module of any generation (a DDR3 server module); DDR5 alone was $7.11. From September 2025 to September 2026 the cheapest DRAM went from $2.12 to $4.85 a GB and the cheapest DDR5 from $2.18 to $13.41. SSD: $0.625 in 2013, $0.0305 in 2023, $0.05 in 2026. The ratio memory over SSD: 7x in 2013, 33x to 57x every year since 2018.
